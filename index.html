SHE TURNS FOUR TONIGHT

Move — WASD or arrow keys
Aim — point your mouse
Flashlight — hold Space (or click). While it's on her, she can't move at all — but the meter drains.
Light a candle — just walk into an unlit one. It catches instantly, no waiting.
Goal — light every candle in the room, then walk through the glowing door.

Level 1 is an easy warm-up — just 2 candles, she starts far away and moves slowly. It gets faster and darker from there.

Touch controls: drag anywhere to move · hold the dial for light.
